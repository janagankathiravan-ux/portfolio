Social experiment · Edit

1 Introvert vs 11 Strangers

The idea
Put one introvert in a room with eleven strangers and film what happens.
The edit
Thirty minutes of raw experiment, three voices woven through it: a narrator who tells you where to look, a coach who explains what's happening, and the introvert saying how it felt. J- and L-cuts carry each voice across the action so the story never stops to explain itself. It became the most-watched video on the channel.
